## 4. Rules for the builders

1. One figure per slide, on at least 80 percent of the slide area.
2. One headline, at most seven words. It names the subject and carries no number that the chart shows.
3. A number appears once in the deck. If Lars says it, it is off the slide.
4. Label the marks directly, once. No legend, no bracket, no caption that repeats a label.
5. No source line on a slide. One line on the last slide covers all figures.
6. No pill, no kicker, no underline, no arrow, no multiplication sign.
7. One accent colour, used for one thing per slide.
8. Motion only where data appears. Text cuts in.
