## Concept

Newspaper graphics desk: paper, ink, orange for marks only, blue for names. Every number is read from `window.TALK`.

Animated elements, all on slide entry or next:

- Slide 1: the amount is typed digit by digit, one stroke underlines it, one orange band pulses once; next click tallies 30 squares, 4 turn orange.
- Slides 2, 6, 11, 12: lines land, a stroke is drawn under one word; the photo wipes in.
- Slide 3: computer and dolls draw themselves.
- Slide 4: 186 dots fill a ledger, total counts up.
- Slides 5, 7, 8: bars grow, shares count up; on 8 bars grow from the year before to now.
- Slide 9: timeline draws, 50 question dots fill.
- Slide 10: next click empties the one dot, the 186 drop.
